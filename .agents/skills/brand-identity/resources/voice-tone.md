# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text or UI components, adhere to this brand persona and visual consistency.

## Brand Personality: Premium Efficiency
* **Professional & Modern:** Clean, minimal, and high-end (Apple-inspired).
* **Direct & Reliable:** Quick responses, automated workflows (like AI question generation).
* **Consistent:** Strictly follows the "Indicator Pattern" (`|`) for all structural labels.

## Visual Voice Rules
* **The Indicator Pattern:** Always prefix structural labels, list items (Sessions/Guidelines), and form section headers with a bold `|` in the appropriate theme color.
* **Solid Clarity:** Maintain a "Solid White" aesthetic (no translucency in main UI areas).
* **Concise Labels:** Keep UI labels short and functional (e.g., "세션명", "담당자 이름").

## Terminology Guide

| Context | Preferred Term | Why? |

| Indicators | "|" | Part of the brand's visual syntax. |
